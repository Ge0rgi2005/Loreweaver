Stories are better when they branch.

Loreweaver is a collaborative home for interactive fiction. Build a story one scene at a time, connect the scenes with choices, and let readers decide where the tale goes.

Weave a world together

Start with a premise, then add scenes and choices to create paths through your story. Keep characters, places, artifacts, events, and factions in the lore codex, and link those entries to the scenes where they appear. A story health check can help you spot unreachable scenes, dead ends, or paths without a reachable ending.

Read your way through

Open a story's reader to follow its starting scene and choose what happens next. Every choice is another thread in the story.
